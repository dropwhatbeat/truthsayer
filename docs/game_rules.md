Here's the game in English — it's called **9upper** (瞎掰王 translates roughly to "Bullshit King" or "Liar King").

**How it works (3–9 players):**

Each round, players are secretly assigned roles:

- **1 Judge** — everyone knows who this is
- **1 Honest Player** — knows the real answer
- **Everyone else = Liars (9uppers)** — must bluff

**Round setup:**
A Fact Card is drawn. The back of the card shows a **term or name** that everyone can see. The front has a description/explanation of that term — but only the Honest Player gets to read the real one.

**The gameplay:**
Each player (except the Judge) takes a turn pretending to read from the card. The Honest Player reads the real text aloud. The Liars have to make something up — they bluff, pretending they're reading the real description.

**The Judge's job:**
After everyone has "read," the Judge has to figure out who was telling the truth. If they pick the Honest Player, they score. If they pick a Liar, the Liars score.

**Scoring:**

- Usually play a set number of rounds, then whoever has the most points wins.

The whole game is about acting convincing and spotting who's faking it — perfect for a variety show segment like the one you're watching!
